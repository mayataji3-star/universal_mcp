# Initial Prediction

**Date:** 6 September 2026

## Viability prediction

My initial prediction is: **viable under specific conditions**.

I believe one MCP layer can expose one common tool for multiple companies that
perform the same business action. However, each company will probably require
an adapter that translates between the common format and its own API.

Provider-specific fields, authentication methods, workflows, errors, and
unsupported capabilities may require custom code.

## Estimated onboarding cost

My initial estimate for onboarding one additional company is:

**24–40 working hours.**

This includes studying the API, mapping fields, implementing the adapter,
configuring authentication, normalizing errors, testing, and documentation.

This prediction was written before beginning the research and implementation.
It will remain unchanged so it can be compared with the final assessment.
